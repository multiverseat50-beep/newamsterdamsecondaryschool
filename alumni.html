Alumni Services

Former students of New Amsterdam Secondary School can now request transcripts and letters of recommendation online through our Alumni Document Request Portal.

Online Alumni Services

School documents made easier to request

Our online service helps former students submit the information needed for the school to locate, verify and process their records.

📄

Transcript Requests

Submit a request for your school transcript and provide your attendance history to assist staff in locating your records.

✉️

Recommendation Letters

Request a recommendation letter for tertiary study, scholarships, employment, training or another official purpose.

🔎

Track Your Request

Use your Request ID and the email address submitted with your application to check the current processing status.

NAMS Alumni Document Request Portal

Ready to submit your request?

Before beginning, have your identification available. If you still have old NAMS report cards, you may also upload clear photographs or scans of report-card pages to assist the school in locating and verifying your academic records more quickly.

Open Alumni Request Portal Contact the School

How it works

The online process is simple and designed to help former students provide the information needed by the school.

1

Open the Portal

Select whether you need a transcript, recommendation letter, or both.

2

Provide Your History

Enter the classes you attended, such as Form 1 – 1L, Form 2 – 2L, and the years where remembered.

3

Upload Identification

Upload a clear identification document. Old report-card pages may also be uploaded if available.

4

Save Your Request ID

After submission, keep your Request ID so you can return to the portal and check your status.

Please note: Providing old report-card pages is optional. Any information supplied will still be checked against the school's official records before a transcript or other official document is issued.
